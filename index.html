Frequently Asked Clinical Questions

Is dental implant surgery painful during or after the procedure?

expand_more
At Khungar Dental Clinic, we utilize computerized local anesthesia and 3D computer-guided surgical guides. The surgery itself is completely painless. Post-operative discomfort is minimal and easily managed with prescribed mild anti-inflammatories.

Can a Root Canal Treatment genuinely be finished in 1 single sitting?

expand_more
Yes! In over 90% of non-acute cases, our motorized rotary endodontic equipment, apex locators, and bio-ceramic sealers enable Dr. Khungar to cleanse, disinfect, and seal the canals thoroughly within a single 45-minute visit.

How do I reach the clinic from Hanumangarh Junction railway station?

expand_more
The clinic is approximately 4.2 km (12 minutes) from Hanumangarh Junction via Town Main Road. Ask for Prem Nagar, Kiryana Bhawan Road, right next to Dindyal Chock.
